## 🛠️ Step 2: Verifying the Administrative Security Firewall

Next, I uploaded a test photo of a cat to check if my background security safety nets were holding up. I hit submit, closed the guest tab, and opened my main host account dashboard to inspect the results.

The test worked flawlessly! The inbound post didn't go live to the public right away. Instead, it was successfully caught and quarantined inside my private administrative hold canvas. 

As shown in the review screenshot below, the post is locked behind a system-enforced **"Awaiting approval"** state. It gives me direct, explicit control tools to either `Reject` or `Approve` the asset before anyone else on the block can see it, proving our data governance filter works beautifully:
