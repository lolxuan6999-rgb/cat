## 🎯 Portfolio Summary: Product Vision & Motivation

### What I Accomplished
I designed and deployed **Neighborhood Cat Journal**, an agile, crowd-sourced digital tracking registry built using no-code architecture. The platform operates as a responsive visual dashboard where local residents can instantly map stray cat sightings, log health statuses, and contribute live field data directly from their mobile web browsers. 

### Strategic Motivation (The "Why")
This project combines a personal dedication to community animal welfare with an exploration of user psychology and conversion funnels. My objective was twofold:
1. **Capitalize on Natural User Behavior:** Tap into the community's organic desire to share pet photos and turn that engagement into an actionable health-and-safety ledger for local strays.
2. **Eliminate Friction at the Gateway:** I engineered an anonymous-first submission workflow because mandatory registration walls act as a major drop-off point for casual users. By sacrificing system-level user profiles, I maximized immediate data collection velocity—ensuring a user standing on a street corner can log an animal profile in under 10 seconds.
