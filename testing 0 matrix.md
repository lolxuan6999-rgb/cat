1. 	The Mobile Data Performance Test (Image Size Check)
The Risk: If a neighbor takes a high-resolution photo on a newer smartphone, the file size could be huge (5MB to 10MB+). If multiple heavy photos are posted, your Padlet board will become incredibly slow to scroll through on mobile data, leading to a frustrating user experience.
How to test it: Use your phone to take a raw, high-resolution photo outside in bright daylight, and upload it as a guest.
What to look for: Does the upload finish quickly over cellular data (4G/5G)? When you open the board link on a secondary phone, does the image pop up immediately, or does the layout lag and stutter while loading?
2. The Duplicate Sighting Test (Comment Log Validation)
The Risk: If two neighbors see the exact same cat ("The Ginger King") at different times, you want to make sure the second neighbor uses the Comment Section rather than making a brand new post that clutters your board.
How to test it: Ask one of your trusted beta-test friends to open the link and pretend they just saw The Ginger King. Have them try to type a comment update (e.g., "Spotted at 5 PM near the trash bins!").
What to look for: Confirm that unregistered guest users can drop comments cleanly under an existing post without being blocked by a sign-up pop-up, and check if their text formats properly.
3. The Visual Privacy Text Scrape (Accidental Address Check)
The Risk: Neighbors often type out exact descriptions out of habit (e.g., "Sitting right outside Blk 420, #03-124"). If you hit approve without checking, you breach local privacy principles.
How to test it: Have your trial partner submit a post containing an explicit house unit number.
What to look for: As the administrator, open your moderation queue and practice editing their text block to erase the exact unit number before you click the green "Approve" button. This ensures you are comfortable with the administrative editing interface.
